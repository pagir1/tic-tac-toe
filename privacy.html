Privacy

Short version: we only save your player name, your email (if you sign in) and your game scores. Nothing else. No ads, no tracking, and we never sell anything.

What we save

  • Your name: the name you choose, shown on leaderboards.
  • Your email: only if you sign in with Google or an email code. It is used to sign you in, and is never shown to other players.
  • Your scores: points from games against the computer, with the game and the date.

Your device also remembers your name, your play streak and your last game, so the home screen can show them. That stays on your phone.

Guest players

If you play as a guest (just a name, no sign-in), your name and scores are deleted automatically after 4 days. Sign in any time before then to keep them.

Saved players

If you sign in, your name and scores are kept so you can play on any device. You can delete your account at any time: tap your profile picture on the home screen, then Delete my account. This removes your name, email and all your scores.

Who can see what

Other players can see your name and points on the leaderboards. Nobody can see your email.

Age

Signing in is for ages 13 and up.

Where it is stored

Accounts and scores are stored with Supabase, our database provider. Google sign-in is handled by Google.

Contact

Questions, or want something removed? Email nepaligamesentertainment@gmail.com.
