How to play Bagh Chal

A traditional game from Nepal. 4 tigers hunt 20 goats.

  • Goats go first. Pieces sit on the points where lines meet and move along the lines.
  • Placing (goats): until all 20 goats are on the board, the goat player places one goat per turn on any empty point. Placed goats can't move yet.
  • Moving (goats): once all 20 are placed, move one goat one step along a line to an empty point.
  • Tigers move one step along a line, or capture by jumping over one goat in a straight line to the empty point right behind it. One capture per turn, and capturing is optional.
  • Diagonal lines only run through some points. If there is no line, you can't move or jump that way.
  • Tigers win by capturing 5 goats, or if goats can't move.
  • Goats win by trapping every tiger so none can move.
  • Tap a piece to see where it can go. Red rings are captures. Tap it again to cancel.
